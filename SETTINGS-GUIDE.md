# Settings guide — what each switch does

Back Office → **Settings**. Change a switch, then press **Save changes** (an "Unsaved changes" hint reminds you).
Tills pick up changes within seconds. Managers and administrators can always do everything; the permission switches are for cashiers.

## Taxes
| Setting | Effect |
|---|---|
| Tax name / Default tax rate | The rate the till starts each sale with (a cashier can still adjust it on the ticket). |
| Prices include tax | **On:** the tax is already inside your prices — totals don't change, tax is shown separately on the receipt. **Off:** tax is added on top. |
| Apply tax to all new products by default | The starting position of **Charge tax on this product** for NEW products (and imports). Each product can be changed. Existing products keep their setting (older ones are all taxed). |

## Receipts
| Setting | Effect |
|---|---|
| Header / footer message | Printed under the business name / at the bottom of every receipt (screen and thermal). Empty footer = "Thank you for shopping with us". |
| Business logo + Show logo | The logo shows on the on-screen/printed receipt when the switch is on. |
| Print automatically | Prints each new sale's receipt right away (USB thermal printer if connected, else the browser print window). Old receipts are never printed automatically. |
| Email receipt button | Adds **Email receipt** to receipts; it opens your email app with the receipt text (to the customer if their email is on file). |
| Show cashier name | Cashier line on screen and thermal receipts. |

## Payment Methods
Cash, Card, Mobile money and **Other** (vouchers, bank transfer, store credit…) decide which buttons appear at checkout. At least one must stay on.
Card/Mobile only *record* how the customer paid. **Split payments** is not available yet.

## Notifications (shown while the Back Office is open)
Low stock alerts (dashboard) · New sale · Refund · Shift opened/closed pop-ups · Daily sales summary (yesterday's sales, once a day).

## Users & Permissions (cashiers)
Apply discounts · Process refunds · View Today's Sales · Change an item's price at checkout · Require a PIN to sign in.

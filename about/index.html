ABOUT AUREUM

A POS and inventory platform for Zimbabwean businesses

From a single bakery counter to several shops under one owner.

What AUREUM is

AUREUM is a point of sale and inventory management platform built for businesses in Zimbabwe. At the counter it is a fast POS that works on a phone, a tablet or a laptop. Behind the counter, the Back Office tracks stock for every shop, records purchases and deliveries, manages staff and shifts, and turns each day's sales into clear reports.

Where it runs

AUREUM runs in a web browser and can be installed like an app on phones, tablets and computers. Sign in once and your business is available on every device you use.

Built for local conditions

Shops work with more than one currency and with payments that happen outside the till, so AUREUM supports an optional second currency and records how each sale was paid — cash, card, mobile money or other.

How AUREUM is built

Made for real shop days

Every cashier works in their own shift, the drawer is counted at the end, receipts match the till, and refunds are done item by item.

Works when the connection doesn't

The till keeps selling without internet. Sales are kept on the device and sync automatically when the connection comes back.

Records you can rely on

Saves that would delete your data are refused, restore points are kept, and every sale stays on record.

One account, several shops

Stock, shifts and sales are kept for each shop, and you can look at one shop or all of them together.

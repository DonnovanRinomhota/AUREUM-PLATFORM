Quick answers

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen choose “Forgot password?”, enter the email on your account and follow the link you receive.

A cashier forgot their PIN.

The owner or an administrator can change it: Back Office → Employees → open the employee → PIN → Save employee. A PIN is exactly 4 digits.

Do my staff need their own accounts?

No. Staff sign in at the POS with their name and PIN. The owner uses the email and password.

Does AUREUM work without internet?

Once opened with internet at least once, yes. The till keeps selling and sales sync automatically when the connection returns.

My printer is not printing.

USB printing needs Chrome or Edge on a computer. Bluetooth printers must be paired to the device first. You can always use the browser’s print option.

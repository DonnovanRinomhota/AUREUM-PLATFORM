How AUREUM works
✕

Ring up a sale

  1. Tap a category, or use the search bar to find an item by name, barcode, or SKU.
  2. Tap a product tile to add it to the ticket — tap again to add more.
  3. Adjust quantity with the – / + controls next to each line item.
  4. Choose a payment method, then tap Complete Sale.

Ticket tools

  • + Add customer — attach the sale to a customer record.
  • + Discount — apply a reduction to the ticket.
  • Hold — park the current ticket and start a new one.
  • Void — clear the ticket and start over.

Payments

  • Cash — use the quick tender chips to enter the amount received; change is calculated automatically.
  • Other — for vouchers, bank transfers, or store credit, type the payment type manually.

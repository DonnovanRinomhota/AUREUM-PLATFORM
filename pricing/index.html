Pricing questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes — 14 days, with every shop included. You can create your account and start straight away.

What happens when the trial ends?

There is a 3-day grace period, then the app locks until you subscribe. Your records are kept. The account owner subscribes from Settings → Billing & Subscription.

How do I pay?

By card, through Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe — AUREUM never stores them. Subscriptions are billed in US dollars.

What if I add or remove a shop?

Adding a shop adds $5 a month — you see the new total and confirm before you are charged, and if the payment fails the shop is not added. Removing a shop lowers your next invoice by $5.

Can I cancel?

Yes. The account owner can manage the card or cancel from Settings → Billing & Subscription. You keep access until the period you have paid for ends.

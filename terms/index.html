Last updated 30 October 2026

These terms apply when you use the AUREUM website, the AUREUM Back Office and the AUREUM POS apps (together, “AUREUM”). By creating an account or using AUREUM you agree to them. If you do not agree, please do not use AUREUM.

Your account

The person who signs up is the account owner and is responsible for the business’s account, for the staff they add, and for keeping passwords and staff PINs private. Please give accurate details and tell us if you think someone has gained access without permission.

Free trial, price and payment

  • Every new account has a 14-day free trial with every feature and every shop included. No card is needed to start.
  • After the trial, AUREUM costs US$5 per shop per month, paid by card through Stripe. Card details are entered on Stripe’s page; AUREUM never sees or stores them.
  • If a payment fails or a subscription ends, access moves to a short grace period of 3 days and then to view-only, so you can still see and export your records.
  • You can change the number of shops or cancel at any time from the Back Office billing screen. Cancelling stops future charges; already-paid periods are not refunded unless the law requires it.
  • If we change the price we will tell account owners inside the app before the change applies.

We may improve, change or remove features, and we will give notice of important changes inside the app. Keep your own regular exports of important records.
